Step 1

Welcome to Bike-MRT

Plan the fastest commute with your trifold bicycle and the MRT/LRT in Singapore.

  1. Choose where from and to. The start is your location by default (the app asks for it when it opens). Type an address, business or station for the destination, tap 📍 and then the map, or pick a favourite: tap ☆ next to the destination to save it, and it shows on the home page. Pins can be dragged.
  2. Tap “Find fastest route”. The app compares cycling the whole way, taking trains, and mixes of both, even cycle → train → cycle → train → cycle.
  3. Read the plan. Green BIKE legs are cycling, coloured MRT / LRT legs are trains, with an estimated fare and the station exits to enter and leave by. Tap a plan to see it on the map.
  4. Navigate each ride. Tap Start navigation: follow the map and the turn-by-turn directions while you cycle, then step through the MRT directions with the arrows and carry on cycling.
  5. Make it yours in ⚙️ Settings. Dark mode, which lines to use, riding speed, longest ride, waiting and transfer times.
📲 Add Bike-MRT to your home screen One tap to open it, full screen like an app, and it works even with a weak signal.

Train times are estimates from distances and LTA's published train frequencies, not live timetables, and routes come from OpenStreetMap, so always check the map. Made with Claude, open source on GitHub.
